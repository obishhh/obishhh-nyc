# Idea: a binder of my real pulls

Somewhere on the site for my favourite real cards I've pulled: a second binder, separate from the post binder.

- **Photos, not rebuilt cards.** Each pocket holds a photo of the actual card I pulled (front, maybe the back),
  in the same binder pockets and sleeves as the post binder, so it reads as the physical collection.
- **Lives beside the post binder.** Its own tab strip or its own page, reached the same way as the other desk
  objects (deck box, Placedex).
- **A little about each pull** (optional): where and when I pulled it, the set, what it's worth to me.

Not started. Needs: where it lives in the UI, how photos are added in the editor's room (likely like Snapshots),
and whether it shares the binder component or gets a variant for photographed cards.
